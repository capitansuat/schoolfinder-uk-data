# Privacy Policy – SchoolFinder UK

**Last updated: 9 May 2026**

SchoolFinder UK ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application SchoolFinder UK ("the App") handles information.

## 1. Information We Collect

SchoolFinder UK does **not** collect, store, or share any personal information from its users.

The App provides a searchable database of UK schools sourced from publicly available datasets (DfE, Ofsted, data.police.uk). All school data is bundled within the App or downloaded from our public GitHub releases repository.

## 2. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:
- Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Location data
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Usage analytics or crash reports sent to us
- Any user-generated content

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## 4. Internet Access

The App may access the internet solely to check for and download updated school database files from our public GitHub releases page (https://github.com/capitansuat/schoolfinder-uk-data/releases). No personal data is transmitted during this process.

## 5. Children's Privacy

SchoolFinder UK is a general information app and does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13. Since we collect no personal data at all, the App is safe for users of all ages.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
